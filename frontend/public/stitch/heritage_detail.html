The Bhairava Window

location_on Patan Durbar Square, Nepal

Period 17th C.
Material Sal Wood
architecture

This intricate wood carving, locally known as a 'Deshay Madu Jhyal' style window, represents the pinnacle of Malla-era artistry. Every inch of the dark sal wood is inhabited by celestial beings, intricate vines, and protective deities designed to shield the sacred space within from malevolent spirits.

history_edu

Historical Context

Carved during the reign of King Siddhinarasimha Malla, these windows were not merely functional. They served as visual scriptures for a largely non-literate population, blending Tantric Buddhism and Hinduism into a singular architectural language.
